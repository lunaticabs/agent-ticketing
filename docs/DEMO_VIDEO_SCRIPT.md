# Presence — demo video script (English, 2 minutes)

**About 210 spoken words.** At a calm 140 words per minute that is roughly 90
seconds of speech, which leaves 30 seconds for the screen to work and for pauses.
Say less; let the demo show more.

Lines in quotes are read aloud. Italics are what the viewer is looking at.

---

## 0:00–0:15 · The problem

*Cold open. No UI — text on black, or a fast scroll of AI activity online.*

> "AI is about to stop answering, and start acting. Booking. Bidding. Buying.
> Within two to three years, it will be doing more online than we do.
>
> So — **who is answerable for what an AI does?**"

## 0:15–0:35 · The demo, in one line

*Cut to the board: the queue, the countdown, the slots.*

> "This demo answers that. One real person asks their agent to buy a ticket online
> — and World ID is what makes that action legitimate."

## 0:35–1:05 · Part one — the harness gives the agent the ability to buy

*The agent transcript scrolling: `tools/list`, `queue.join`, `queue.status`.*

> "Part one. The harness gives the agent the ability to buy tickets, through an
> MCP server. Three tools: join the queue, check the status, claim the slot.
>
> The agent does the waiting. The agent does the polling. The agent does the
> buying."

## 1:05–1:25 · Part two — a simulated ticketing system

*The draw settles. A slot tile appears with its countdown.*

> "Part two: a ticketing system we simulated — an event, a queue, a draw, and a
> handful of slots."

## 1:25–1:50 · The moment that matters

*The agent stops. The approval screen. A phone in hand. Then the audit ticker.*

> "And then the agent stops, and asks the human.
>
> They approve on their own phone — present, identified, right now, for this exact
> ticket. The agent finishes the purchase.
>
> Four actions by the agent. One by the human. And it is the one thing an agent
> cannot do for itself."

## 1:50–2:00 · Close

*Hold on the audit ticker: the `[agent]` lines, and the single `[human]` line.*

> "The agent does the work. A real person is accountable.
>
> **That is World ID for agents.**"

---

## If you have to cut

Cut in this order: the second and third sentences of 0:35–1:05, then all of
1:05–1:25. Never cut the question at 0:15, and never cut the last line.
